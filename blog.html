But it seems like after 2014, the whole human race instantaneously agreed that the world should look more boring and dull in order to be more appealing.

I find the weirdest thing about my strange obsession with old things to be the sudden excitement I get from clicking glossy 3D buttons, staring at skeuomorphic interfaces, playing pixelated and low poly video games.
